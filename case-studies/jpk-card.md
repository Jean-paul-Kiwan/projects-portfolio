# JPK Card — Interactive Digital Business Card

**Context:** web-development internship at Questa.  
**Project technologies:** React, JavaScript, and browser-based augmented reality.

## Overview

Developed an interactive digital business-card application combining a web interface with augmented-reality functionality.

The project connects frontend development with an interactive browser-based presentation of a digital card, rather than a conventional static contact page.
